FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to apply for a loan?

For most loans: PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, 3 months of bank statements, and income proof (salary slips or ITR). We share an exact checklist based on your loan type.

Do you charge for financial consultation?

No. The initial consultation is completely free. You only pay for products or services you choose to opt in for.

How long does loan approval take?

Personal loans typically get approved within 24–72 hours. Home and business loans usually take 5–10 working days depending on documentation.

Which insurance providers do you work with?

We partner with major IRDAI-approved insurers, which lets us compare plans across companies and recommend suitable options.
